# Sound credits

Every sound here is built by `tools/build-sfx.py` from recordings released into the public domain
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution isn't required, but credit is due:

| Sounds in the app | Source |
| --- | --- |
| `tap`, `select`, `tick`, `toggle`, `success`, `celebrate`, `unlock`, `open`, `error` | [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) by Kenney |
| `pour` (splash layer) | [40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx) by rubberduck |
| `pour` (bubble layers), `boop`, `swim` | [Bubble sound effects](https://opengameart.org/content/bubble-sound-effects) by BMacZero, plus `bubble_02` from rubberduck's pack |

Numbered files (`pour-1`, `pour-2`, …) are the same sound at slightly different pitches or with
different layers. The app picks one at random each time so repeats sound natural.
